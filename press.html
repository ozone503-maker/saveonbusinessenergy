About

Save on Business Energy is an independent lead generator that helps Texas businesses get a free review of their commercial electricity rate and contract. Brokerage services are provided through USA Energy Partners, a PUCT-registered broker. We are not a retail electric provider. There is no fee billed to the business for the review.

Our View on Efficient Growth

Texas data-center growth is real and is reshaping interconnection and planning. We support projects that are efficient, transparent about their power and water plans, and compatible with a reliable grid that serves existing businesses. The practical step any business can take today is to understand its own rate, demand charges, and contract end date so it is not surprised when the fixed term ends.
