What "efficient" means here

Not less power. Less waste.

Efficient does not mean using less power. It means not wasting money on the power you use.

A competitive rate for how your business actually uses power. A contract that fits, with the end date known.

No surprise when the fixed term ends and the rate jumps to a holdover rate.
