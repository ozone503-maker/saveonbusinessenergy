What happens if your REP goes out of business?

It happens. A retail electric provider exits the market, and its customers need somewhere to go. Texas built a safety net for exactly that. Your lights stay on.

If your provider goes out of business, the lights stay on — you are moved automatically to a backup provider, and then you shop for a new plan.

The safety net

Provider of Last Resort

Texas has a Provider of Last Resort, or POLR. If your retail electric provider cannot continue service, you are transferred to a designated POLR.

The PUCT designates Providers of Last Resort as backup electric service providers in each area of Texas open to competition. The largest providers in each delivery area are required to serve as POLRs.

No interruption

There is no interruption in service. Your TDU keeps delivering power while the transfer happens.

The name on your bill changes. The lights do not go out.

Source: PUCT Substantive Rule 25.43, Provider of Last Resort service.

What happens, step by step

  • You get notice that your provider is leaving and you are being moved to the POLR.
  • You are transferred to the designated POLR in your area. Service continues.
  • You choose a new provider and sign a new plan.

Why you should not stay on POLR

POLR rates run high

POLR service is relatively high-priced. Providers have to plan for uncertainty and take on the risk of serving an unknown number of customers with uncertain usage.

That is why POLR is a safety net, not a plan. It exists to keep your electricity on while you choose your next provider.

What to do

  • Do not panic. Your power stays on.
  • Read the notice. It names your POLR and your options.
  • Shop for a new provider right away. Do not wait out the POLR rate.

FAQ

Will my power be cut off?

No. POLR service is designed to prevent an interruption in electric service when a provider exits the market. Your TDU keeps delivering power.

Do I have to stay with the POLR?

No. You are free to choose another retail electric provider. POLR is meant to keep your lights on temporarily while you enroll with another provider or switch to a new plan.
